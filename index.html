Outback Motocross Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Outback Motocross ("the game") is a motocross racing game for iPhone made by Phong Bui ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. You don't need an account to play, and I don't ask you for your name, email address or any other personal details.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only: the tracks you've unlocked and your best times and stars, your coins, your bikes, upgrades and paints, the riders and outfits you own, your settings, and which purchases you own. This stays on your phone. I do not receive it. If you delete the game, it is removed.

To stop saved games being edited to cheat, the game protects your save file using an identifier from your device. This happens on your phone only, and the identifier is never sent anywhere.

Checking the time

To stop the daily free coins from being skipped by changing the phone's clock, the game briefly asks a public web server (www.apple.com) for the current date and time. This is a normal web request. It sends no personal details beyond your IP address, which any website you visit receives.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your best race times and your total stars to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, so you can compare them with other players. Your Game Center name and scores are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent.

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. Ads appear between some races, and as optional rewarded videos when you choose to watch one for free coins in the Store or to double a race prize. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device identifiers (for example the advertising identifier, if you allow it), IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for your consent before ads are loaded, and you can change your choice later in the game's Settings screen under Ad privacy choices.

Learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

On iPhone the game asks whether you allow it to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites to show more relevant ads. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are still shown, but they are less personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Purchases

The game offers optional purchases: Remove Ads, packs of coins, the Starter Pack, world bundles (tracks, paint and coins), All Riders, and outfits for Sam. These are handled entirely by Apple. I do not see or store your payment details. Apple tells the game whether a purchase has been made, so it can be delivered, and so items you bought once can be restored on a new device.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing

I do not sell your information. The only parties that receive data because of the game are Google (for ads) and Apple (for purchases, Game Center and the time check), as described above.

Your choices

  • Say no, or later withdraw, consent for ads where you are asked.
  • Limit ad tracking in your iPhone settings.
  • Buy Remove Ads to stop the ads shown between races. Rewarded videos stay optional.
  • Sign out of Game Center if you don't want your scores on the leaderboards.
  • Delete the game to remove everything it stores on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date above.
